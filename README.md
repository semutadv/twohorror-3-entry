Exactly two sentences, up to 400 characters: the scariest story that fits.
This entry spends its first sentence on a voice coming through a baby
monitor, gentle and practised — and its second on the two facts that make
the first one unbearable: the narrator is already holding the baby, and
lives alone.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the story to `entry.txt` in the
output directory, clearing the directory's contents first and adding no
trailing newline.
